Play comfortably

Freefall is best played seated, ideally in a swivel chair, with headphones. You never need to walk; everything you grab is within arm's reach. The game contains fast motion — free fall and flight under canopy. If you start to feel unwell, stop, take the headset off and rest. By default you steer by turning your head and body, so the world never spins around you; leave Canopy steering on that setting if you are sensitive to motion.

Common questions

How do I jump?
Pick a world in the start window and choose Jump. You start in your own room with a yellow bar beside you: grab it (make a fist or pinch) and the plane grows around you. When the jump light turns green, let go.
How do I open my parachute?
The altimeter on your wrist alarms at 3,500 feet. With the watch hand, grab the red handle on your other forearm, near the wrist, and pull it outward. Pull before about 550 feet, or the canopy won't have time to open.
How do I steer and land?
Under canopy, turn your head and body and the canopy follows you. Grab the yellow toggles beside your head and pull both down to slow down, or push them forward to dive. Land inside the red-and-white circle to win; the yellow bullseye in the middle is a perfect landing.
How do I see my altitude and the landing spot?
Tap the watch on your wrist with your other hand's index finger to show or hide the helmet display. Hold the watch for two seconds to quit a jump; pressing the Digital Crown also exits.
How do I unlock canopies and gloves?
Every world has its own canopy, and its own gloves and sleeves. Land on a world's target to unlock its canopy; land on the bullseye to unlock its gloves and sleeves. Nothing is sold.
Why does it need hand tracking?
You grab the bar, pull the handle and the toggles with your real hands, and the altimeter is worn on your wrist. Hand data never leaves your device — see the privacy policy. Freefall can't be played without it: if it is off, the start window shows Open Settings — allow hand tracking for Freefall there and come back.
How do I change the language?
In the start window's settings. Freefall is in English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano and Português (Brasil).
Is my progress saved?
Your closest landing in each world, the gear you have unlocked and your settings are saved on your device. To start over from nothing, delete Freefall and install it again.
Can I get a refund?
Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reporting a bug

The most useful report says what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. The world, the altitude and whether the canopy was open usually narrow it down straight away.

舒适地玩

建议坐着玩（最好是能转的椅子），戴上耳机。全程不需要走动，要抓的东西都在手边。 游戏里有快速的运动——自由落体和伞下飞行。如果感到不适，请停下来，摘下头显休息。 默认靠转头、转身来转向，世界不会绕着你转；容易晕的话，「伞下转向」请保持默认设置。

常见问题

怎么跳？
在开始窗口选一关，点「跳」。一开始你在自己的房间里，身边立着一根黄色扶杆：握住它（握拳或捏住都行）， 飞机就会在你身边长出来。跳伞灯变绿以后松手。
怎么开伞？
手腕上的高度表在 3,500 英尺报警。用戴表的那只手抓住另一只小臂上靠近手腕的红色拉环，往外拉。 要在 550 英尺左右以上拉，不然伞来不及张开。
怎么转向和降落？
伞下转头、转身，伞就跟着你转。抓住头两侧的黄色操纵环，两边一起往下拉是减速，往前推是俯冲。 落进地上红白相间的圆圈就赢，正中的黄色靶心是完美着陆。
怎么看高度和降落点？
用另一只手的食指点一下手腕上的表，打开或关上头盔 HUD。按住手表两秒退出这一跳；按数码表冠也能退出。
降落伞和手套怎么解锁？
每一关都有自己的降落伞和一套手套、袖子。落进这一关的降落点解锁它的伞，落进靶心再解锁它的手套和袖子。不卖任何东西。
为什么需要手部追踪？
你要用真手抓扶杆、拉拉环和操纵环，高度表也戴在手腕上。手部数据不会离开你的设备， 详见隐私政策。没有手部追踪就玩不了：没允许时开始窗口会显示「打开设置」，在设置里允许 Freefall 使用手部追踪后回来即可。 想重新允许，可以在「设置」app 的「隐私与安全性」里打开。
怎么换语言？
在开始窗口的设置里。支持十种语言。
进度会保存吗？
每一关最近的落点、解锁的装备和你的设置都保存在设备上。想完全重新开始，删除 Freefall 后重新安装即可。
能退款吗？
购买通过 Apple 完成，退款也通过 Apple：reportaproblem.apple.com。
