Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Freefall does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. The game contains no networking code, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not require an account. Nothing you do in the game leaves your device.

Hand tracking

With your permission, Freefall reads the positions of your hands and fingers through Apple's ARKit, so you can grab the bar, pull the handle and the toggles, and wear the altimeter on your wrist. This happens on your Apple Vision Pro, frame by frame, in memory. Hand data is never recorded, stored or transmitted. Freefall is played with your own hands, so it needs hand tracking to play.

Head position

The game uses the position and direction of your head, provided by visionOS, to place the plane, the canopy and the helmet display around you. It is used only while you play and is never stored or transmitted.

Where you look

visionOS does not give apps information about where your eyes are looking, and Freefall never receives it.

Game progress and settings

Your settings, your closest landing in each world and the gear you have unlocked are saved in the game's own private storage on your device. Freefall does not upload them anywhere. If you use iCloud Backup, they may be included in your device's backup, which Apple manages.

Diagnostics

The game sends no crash reports or usage data. If you have chosen in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with the developer; that is Apple's setting, and you can turn it off at any time. If you contact me by email, only what you choose to write is shared.

Children

Freefall collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Everything the game stores lives inside the app. Deleting Freefall from your device removes all of it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 13 日

Freefall 不收集、不存储、不传输、也不分享任何个人数据。 游戏里没有任何联网代码，没有统计分析、广告或追踪，也不需要账号。你在游戏里做的任何事都不会离开你的设备。

手部追踪

经你允许后，游戏通过 Apple 的 ARKit 读取双手和手指的位置，让你能抓扶杆、拉开伞拉手和操纵环，并把高度表戴在手腕上。 这些都在你的 Apple Vision Pro 上逐帧在内存中完成，手部数据不会被记录、存储或传输。游戏要用你自己的手来玩，所以需要允许手部追踪才能玩。

头部位置

游戏使用 visionOS 提供的头部位置和朝向，把飞机、降落伞和头盔显示摆在你周围。只在游玩时使用，不会被存储或传输。

你看向哪里

visionOS 不向任何 app 提供眼睛注视的位置，Freefall 也从未获得这类信息。

游戏进度和设置

你的设置、每一关最近的落点和已解锁的装备保存在设备上游戏自己的私有存储里，Freefall 不会把它们上传到任何地方。 如果你开启了 iCloud 云备份，它们可能包含在由 Apple 管理的设备备份中。

诊断信息

游戏不发送崩溃报告或使用数据。如果你在设备设置中选择了与 app 开发者共享分析数据，Apple 可能会把匿名的崩溃报告提供给开发者； 这是 Apple 的设置，你可以随时关闭。如果你通过邮件联系我，只会分享你自己写下的内容。

儿童

Freefall 不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

删除数据

游戏存储的一切都在 app 里面，从设备上删除 Freefall 就会全部删除。

变更

如果本政策有变化，更新后的版本会发布在这个页面上，并在上方标注新的日期。
